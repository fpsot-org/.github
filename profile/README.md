# FPSOT – Foundation for Public Sector Open Technology

> **Where Public Sector Meets Open Innovation.**

The **Foundation for Public Sector Open Technology (FPSOT)** is a non-profit initiative dedicated to helping governments and public institutions adopt, build, and sustain **free and open technologies**.

We believe that public services should be powered by **transparent, interoperable, and community-owned software**.

---

## What we do

- 🏛️ **Support the public sector**  
  Provide guidance, reference architectures, and best practices for governments that want to adopt open source and open standards.

- 🧩 **Build and maintain open technology**  
  Develop reusable, government-grade open source components and reference implementations.

- 📜 **Advocate for policy change**  
  Promote procurement, funding, and legal frameworks that prioritize open technologies and digital sovereignty.

- 🎓 **Educate and connect**  
  Create learning resources, examples, and communities of practice for public servants, vendors, and open-source contributors.

---

## Initial focus areas

- **Reference architectures** for modern public-sector systems  
- **Policy & procurement templates** for FOSS-friendly RFPs  
- **Sample projects** showing how to build “government-ready” systems using open technologies  
- **Playbooks** for migrating from proprietary stacks to open source
