# FPSOT – Foundation for Public Sector Open Technology

> **Where Public Sector Meets Open Innovation.**

The **Foundation for Public Sector Open Technology (FPSOT)** is a non-profit initiative helping governments and public institutions adopt, build, and sustain free and open technologies. We believe public services should run on software that is transparent, interoperable, and community-owned. FPSOT works at both ends of that problem: the policy and procurement frameworks that make open technology adoptable, and the code and reference implementations that make it deployable.

---

## What we do

- 🏛️ **Support the public sector**
  Guidance, reference architectures, and best practices for governments adopting open source and open standards.

- 🧩 **Build and maintain open technology**
  Reusable, government-grade open source components and reference implementations.

- 📜 **Advocate for policy change**
  Procurement, funding, and legal frameworks that prioritize open technologies and digital sovereignty.

- 🎓 **Educate and connect**
  Learning resources, worked examples, and communities of practice for public servants, vendors, and open-source contributors.

## What we're working on first

- **Reference architectures** for modern public-sector systems
- **Policy & procurement templates** for FOSS-friendly RFPs
- **Sample projects** showing how to build "government-ready" systems with open technologies
- **Migration playbooks** for moving from proprietary stacks to open source
